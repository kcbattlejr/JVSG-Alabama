## 4. Significant contribution in information technology

> Alabama's Jobs for Veterans State Grant (JVSG) team has developed a repeatable
> way to turn recurring program-report exports into a staff-facing follow-up
> dashboard. The workflow loads and validates report files, transforms structured
> data with deterministic rules, and presents cohort, office, and follow-up
> information in familiar Excel views. A structured profile supports freshness
> indicators and a fixed-template referral brief for qualified staff review.
>
> The contribution is a practical ETL and data-visualization approach built around
> existing tools, repeatable refreshes, and human oversight. Staff retain
> responsibility for interpreting records and making decisions; GeoSol remains
> the system of record. The current demonstration uses no SLM or other model,
> does not mine case notes, and does not send referrals automatically. The work
> is a prototype, not an approved production service.

## 5. Implementation timeline and accomplishments

> **November 2026:** Built the report-import and validation workflow, deterministic
> transformations, and Excel dashboard views for management summaries and staff
> follow-up. Added synthetic fixtures and documentation for reproducing the
> refresh and reviewing data boundaries.
>
> **November 30, 2026:** `[After the demonstration, add the presentation date,
> confirmed demo scope, and approved stakeholder sign-off.]`
>
> **Data handling:** All development, testing, and debugging used synthetic
> data. The workflow now runs on live veteran data inside the agency's
> access-controlled JVSG SharePoint site; no veteran data appears in the source
> repository or in any public-facing material. Running on live data does not by
> itself establish accuracy validation, production approval, service outcomes,
> or time savings, and none is claimed.

## 6. Contribution to the workforce development community and state

> The dashboard is intended to give JVSG staff a clearer, more consistent view of
> follow-up workload and recorded information, while reducing manual preparation.
> These are expected benefits, not measured results. A future, separately
> authorized pilot would evaluate preparation time, data corrections, and timely
> follow-up against the current process.
>
> The approach may be useful to other state workforce agencies that work with
> recurring report exports: validate inputs, apply transparent rules, visualize
> the results in familiar tools, and keep people responsible for interpretation
> and action. The project is documented as a transferable method rather than a
> production product. No national impact or adoption by other states is claimed.
