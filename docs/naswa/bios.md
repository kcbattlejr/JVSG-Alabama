## Keith Battle — Project lead and developer

Keith Battle is a Workforce Specialist – Veteran Services with the Alabama
Department of Workforce in Mobile, serving veterans since September 2025. With
Robert Stratton he received the JVSG Team Incentive Award in October 2026, the
team's first year of service to the state. In the first three quarters of 2026,
the Mobile JVSG team he serves on reported about $2.24 million in combined
annualized wages for veterans entering employment ($988,795 in Q1, about $474K
in Q2 and about $777K in Q3; Q4 not yet reported; MIS Entered Employment data).
He designed and built the JVSG follow-up triage workflow, from the SQL extracts
through the SharePoint and Excel presentation layer.

His background combines operations and research computing: IT management at a
Gulf Coast company, research technologist work at the Alabama Supercomputer
Authority, and graduate research at the University of South Alabama's Whiddon
College of Medicine. He holds a B.A.S. from the University of South Alabama
(2016), is completing an M.S. in Data Science at Western Governors University
(expected Dec 2026) with WGU certificates in Data Analytics and Data
Operations, and holds a Certified in Cybersecurity credential. He helped
organize the 2026 Veterans Gulf Coast Job Fair.

## Hank Lahti — State Coordinator, Jobs for Veterans State Grants

Hank Lahti is the State Coordinator for the Jobs for Veterans State Grants
program in Alabama. He previously served three years as Assistant State
Coordinator and six years as a Local Veterans' Employment Representative, after
a period as a Business Services Representative. Before joining workforce
services he owned and operated an asset-protection business for more than 14
years. He is a U.S. Coast Guard aviation mechanic veteran, a graduate of
Northern Arizona University, and a Lions Club volunteer.

His program expertise shaped the case-note population definition, the
follow-up rules, and the referral-profile questions in this project.

## Greg Reed — Secretary of Workforce, State of Alabama

Greg Reed is the first Secretary of Workforce for the State of Alabama, leading
the Alabama Department of Workforce at the request of Governor Kay Ivey. Before
that, as a member of the Alabama Senate, he sponsored legislation focused on
workforce transformation and economic development, including the 2019 Alabama
Incentives Modernization Act, renewal of the Growing Alabama Act and the Alabama
Jobs Act in 2023, and in 2024 the Working for Alabama package and the Alabama
Growth Alliance Act.
