## Keith Battle — Lead Architect and Developer

Keith Battle serves as a Workforce Specialist – Veteran Services with the Alabama Department of Workforce in Mobile, supporting transitioning service members and veterans since September 2025. Alongside Mobile JVSG teammate Robert Stratton, he received the JVSG Team Incentive Award in October 2026 during the team's inaugural year of service. Across the first three quarters of 2026, their two-person Mobile JVSG unit reported approximately $2.24 million in combined annualized wages for veterans entering employment ($988,795 in Q1, $474,801 in Q2, and $777,886 in Q3; Q4 pending; MIS Entered Employment data). Drawing on frontline intake-triage challenges, Battle architected and built the JVSG follow-up visibility pipeline—from read-only SQL extraction through SharePoint governance and the Excel Online presentation layer.

Battle brings a multidisciplinary background spanning IT operations and scientific computing, including prior roles in IT management on the Gulf Coast, as a Research Technologist at the Alabama Supercomputer Authority, and in graduate research at the University of South Alabama's Whiddon College of Medicine. He holds a B.A.S. from the University of South Alabama (2016), is completing an M.S. in Data Science at Western Governors University (expected December 2026) alongside WGU certificates in Data Analytics and Data Operations, and holds the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) credential. He also helped organize the 2026 Veterans Gulf Coast Job Fair.

## Henry "Hank" Lahti — State Coordinator, Jobs for Veterans State Grants

Henry "Hank" Lahti serves as the State Coordinator for the Jobs for Veterans State Grants (JVSG) program at the Alabama Department of Workforce. Prior to assuming statewide leadership, he served three years as Assistant State Coordinator, six years as a Local Veterans' Employment Representative (LVER), and as a Business Services Representative. Before entering public workforce development, he owned and operated an asset-protection enterprise for more than 14 years. A U.S. Coast Guard aviation mechanic veteran, Lahti holds a degree from Northern Arizona University and is an active Lions Club volunteer.

As program owner for this initiative, Lahti's operational expertise defined the case-note cohort rules, contact-timeliness thresholds, and structured barrier-referral framework.

## Kevin Kidd — Executive Sponsor

Kevin Kidd serves as Director of the Employment Services Division at the Alabama Department of Workforce (appointed July 2024), having previously served as Program Services Manager (2021–2025). A veteran public administrator with the Alabama Career Center System since 1998, Kidd progressed through frontline and leadership roles including Employment Security Representative, Supervisor, Employment Services Manager, and Area Field Services Supervisor. He holds a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Alabama and provides executive sponsorship and operational oversight for Alabama's JVSG modernization efforts.

## Greg Reed — Secretary of Workforce, State of Alabama

Greg Reed serves as Alabama's first Secretary of Workforce, leading the Alabama Department of Workforce at the appointment of Governor Kay Ivey. Prior to leading the department, Reed served in the Alabama Senate, where he championed landmark workforce transformation and economic development legislation—including the 2019 Alabama Incentives Modernization Act, the 2023 renewals of the Growing Alabama Act and Alabama Jobs Act, and the 2024 Working for Alabama legislative package and Alabama Growth Alliance Act.
